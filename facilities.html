Our School Facilities

Classrooms

The classrooms at Rwenkobwa Secondary School provide a clean and comfortable environment for students to learn.
They are arranged to support teaching, discussion and group activities.
The classrooms help students concentrate on their studies and develop knowledge, skills and good learning habits.

Laboratory

The laboratory at Rwenkobwa Secondary School provides students with a suitable environment for conducting science experiments and practical lessons.
It helps students understand scientific concepts through hands-on activities, observation and research.
The laboratory supports the teaching of subjects such as Biology, Chemistry and Physics.

Library

The library at Rwenkobwa Secondary School provides students with a quiet and suitable environment for reading and studying.
It contains learning materials and reference books that support students in their academic work.
The library also encourages a culture of reading, research, self-study and continuous learning.

Dormitories

The dormitory at Rwenkobwa Secondary School provides students with a comfortable and organized place to stay during their school life.
It promotes discipline, cleanliness, cooperation and responsibility among students.
The dormitory environment also helps students develop good habits while enjoying time with their fellow students.

Main Hall

The main hall at Rwenkobwa Secondary School is an important place for school gatherings and activities.
It provides space for assemblies, meetings, celebrations, presentations, prayers and other school events.
The hall brings students and staff together and provides a suitable environment for learning, sharing ideas and building teamwork.

compound

Rwenkobwa Secondary School has a clean, green and
well-organized compound that provides a peaceful environment for learning.
The compound has beautiful gardens, trees, flowers, pathways, classrooms and other school buildings.
Students can enjoy a safe and comfortable environment while studying and taking part in school activities.
