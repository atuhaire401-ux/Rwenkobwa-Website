School Facilities

School Buildings

Our school buildings provide facilities for learning and administration.

Classrooms

Our classrooms provide a suitable learning environment.

Computer Laboratory

Students learn computer and ICT skills using the computer laboratory.

Main hall

The main hall is an important place at
Rwenkobwa Secondary School where students
and staff gather for different activities.
It is used for school meetings, assemblies, celebrations, presentations, seminars, and other important events. The hall provides a spacious and comfortable environment where students can learn, interact, and share ideas. It also promotes unity, teamwork, and cooperation among students and staff.

Domitory

At Rwenkobwa Secondary School, dormitories provide students with
a safe, clean, and comfortable place to rest after classes.
Dormitories also help students develop important values such as
responsibility, cooperation, cleanliness, and respect.
