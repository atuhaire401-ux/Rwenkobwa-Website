School Activities

Music, Dance & Drama

Music, Dance and Drama (MDD) is an important part of life at Rwenkobwa Secondary School.
It gives students an opportunity to discover, develop and showcase their talents through singing, dancing, acting and other forms of creative performance.
Students are encouraged to participate in MDD activities both inside and outside the classroom. Through Music, Dance and Drama, students develop confidence, creativity, teamwork and communication skills. They learn how to work together during rehearsals, respect different talents, and perform confidently before an audience. These activities also provide students with a good way of expressing their ideas, feelings and experiences. The school supports students in developing their talents by providing opportunities to participate in school celebrations, competitions, assemblies and other special events.
Students can take part in different performances and learn from one another while improving their skills. Music, Dance and Drama also helps students to appreciate culture and traditions.
Through songs, dances and plays, students can learn about important values and different aspects of society.
At Rwenkobwa Secondary School, MDD contributes to the development of well-rounded students who can combine academic education with creativity, talent and personal development.

Debate

Debate at Rwenkobwa Secondary School Debate is one of the important activities at Rwenkobwa Secondary School.
It provides students with an opportunity to discuss different topics, share their ideas, and learn from one another.
Through debate, students are encouraged to research different subjects and present their views clearly and confidently before their fellow students and teachers. At Rwenkobwa Secondary School, debates help students improve their communication, public speaking, listening, and critical thinking skills.
Students learn how to give strong reasons and examples to support their ideas while also respecting the opinions of others.
Debate also helps students overcome fear of speaking in front of people and prepares them to communicate confidently in school and in their future careers. The debate activities encourage teamwork because students work together to prepare arguments and understand the topics being discussed.
They also teach students the importance of discipline, patience, good listening, and respectful communication.
Through regular participation in debates, students become more confident, informed, and responsible members of the school community. Rwenkobwa Secondary School continues to encourage students to take part in debates
because they are not only educational but also enjoyable and useful in developing students' talents and abilities.

Environmental Activities

Environment activities at Rwenkobwa Secondary School help students understand the importance of protecting and caring for their surroundings.
Students take part in activities such as tree planting, cleaning the school compound, maintaining gardens, proper waste disposal, and keeping the environment clean and beautiful. These activities teach students about environmental conservation and the need to protect natural resources.
They also encourage teamwork, responsibility, discipline, and good hygiene.
Through environmental activities, students learn that everyone has a role to play in creating a clean, healthy, and sustainable environment for both the school and the wider community.

Community Service

Community service is an important activity at Rwenkobwa Secondary School that encourages students to give back to the community and develop a spirit of responsibility.
Students participate in activities such as cleaning public areas, helping with environmental conservation, supporting community projects, and taking part in activities that benefit people around the school. Through community service, students learn the importance of teamwork, kindness, cooperation, leadership, and responsibility.
They are encouraged to identify challenges in their communities and take part in finding practical solutions.
These activities also help students understand that education is not only about classroom learning but also about using their knowledge and skills to make a positive contribution to society. Rwenkobwa Secondary School encourages community service because it helps students become responsible, caring, and active members of their communities.

Clubs & Societies

Clubs and societies are an important part of student life at Rwenkobwa Secondary School.
They provide students with opportunities to discover their talents, develop new skills, share ideas, and participate in activities outside the normal classroom lessons.
Students are encouraged to join clubs and societies according to their interests and abilities. Through these activities, students develop leadership, teamwork, communication, creativity, and problem-solving skills.
Clubs and societies also help students make new friends, build confidence, and learn how to work together towards common goals.
Activities may include debates, music, sports, environmental conservation, entrepreneurship, and other educational and cultural activities. The school encourages active participation in clubs and societies because they contribute to the overall development of students.
They help learners become confident, responsible, creative, and well-rounded individuals who can make a positive contribution to the school and the wider community.

Competitions

Competitions are an important part of student life at Rwenkobwa Secondary School.
They give students an opportunity to demonstrate their knowledge, talents, creativity, and skills in different areas.
Students participate in activities such as academic competitions, sports, debates, Music, Dance and Drama, quizzes, and other school activities. Through competitions, students learn the importance of teamwork, discipline, hard work, confidence, and good sportsmanship.
They are encouraged to prepare well, respect their opponents, and learn from both success and challenges.
Competitions also provide a platform for students to discover and develop their talents. Rwenkobwa Secondary School encourages students to take part in different competitions because they make school life more interesting and help learners develop skills that are useful both in school and in the wider community.
