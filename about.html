About Rwenkobwa Secondary School

Who We Are

Rwenkobwa Secondary School is dedicated to providing quality education in a safe, disciplined and supportive environment.


Our school aims at developing learners academically, socially, morally and physically.


Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in education, discipline and holistic development.


Our Mission

To provide quality education and equip learners with knowledge, skills and values for responsible citizenship.


Our Values

• Discipline

• Integrity

• Respect

• Hard work

• Teamwork

• Excellence

Our Commitment

We are committed to supporting every learner to discover their potential and become a responsible member of society.
