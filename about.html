About Our School

Rwenkobwa Secondary School is a learning institution committed to providing quality education and developing students academically, socially, and morally. Our school provides a friendly and supportive environment where students are encouraged to discover their talents, build confidence, and prepare for a successful future. We believe in discipline, hard work, respect, teamwork, and academic excellence.
Through classroom learning, sports, co-curricular activities, and guidance from our dedicated staff, we help students become responsible and productive members of society. Rwenkobwa Secondary School — Educating, Inspiring and Building the Future.

The school promotes academic excellence, discipline, leadership, teamwork and responsible citizenship.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that develops knowledgeable, disciplined and responsible citizens.

Our Vision

To become a centre of excellence in education and character development.
